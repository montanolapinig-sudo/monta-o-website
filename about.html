Our Story

Forma House Gym was founded by Christle Joyce L. Montaño out of a deep desire to break down the walls of judgment and high costs in the fitness industry.

Many people feel scared or intimidated by high-end gyms. Here, we built a supportive family where everyone is treated fairly and with love.
